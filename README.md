## Featured Projects

### [CLIMA TERRA — for people](https://clima-terra.netlify.app/)

An evolving digital experience designed to bring climate and environmental information closer to everyday life.

CLIMA TERRA helps people explore conditions such as temperature, UV radiation, humidity, wind, rain and air quality through accessible, contextual and useful information.

### [ATLAS — for all](https://atlas-for-all.netlify.app/)

An evolving global interactive experience for exploring natural events and phenomena around the world.

ATLAS brings together information from authoritative sources to help people discover and understand earthquakes, wildfires, tropical cyclones and volcanic activity through an interactive 3D globe.

## How I Work

I approach each project through curiosity, research, experimentation and continuous learning.

I believe meaningful digital experiences should be:

- **Clear** — making ideas and information easy to understand.
- **Visual** — using design and storytelling to communicate meaning.
- **Interactive** — encouraging exploration, participation and discovery.
- **Useful** — creating value beyond technological novelty.
- **Accessible** — considering different people, contexts and devices.
- **Transparent** — communicating sources, limitations and relevant information.
- **Adaptable** — choosing technologies and formats according to each project's purpose.
- **Continuously evolving** — improving through research, feedback and experimentation.

I combine AI-assisted creation with conventional tools and technologies, always looking for practical, creative and sustainable ways to bring ideas to life.
